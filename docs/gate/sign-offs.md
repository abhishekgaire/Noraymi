## Applying an answer

1. Keep the adviser's written answer (an email or a signed letter) outside the repo, with nothing about a guest in it, and note where here.
2. Add a decision row to [decisions](../decisions.md) naming the question and the answer, and move the question to Closed in [Open technical questions](../spec/14-open-questions.md), in the same commit.
3. Apply it as the row says. Where the answer is the cautious default, nothing changes and the decision row says the default stands. Where it differs, the setting, rule-pack version or ticket switches away from the default, with its tests.
4. Set the row's Status, date and decision. When all ten are `applied`, change gate item 2 above to met, with the date.

The founder's own gate questions (the on-call responders, and who runs the breach runbook) aren't advisers' sign-offs; they're tracked in M9-16's rota and [breach contacts](../security/breach-contacts.md).
