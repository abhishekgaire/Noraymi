## Owner onboarding: "Set up your venue"

Oct 11, 2026 · how a new venue gets from "we made your account" to its first live night without our team typing its facts in for it ([D113](../decisions.md)). The venue's owner runs a wizard in the staff app; every answer is saved through the same routes and settings keys Admin already uses, so the wizard is only a guided order over Admin, and anything entered here can be changed later in Admin by the people allowed to change it there. The look comes from the frozen canvas [`Setup.dc.html`](../../design/canvas/Setup.dc.html) ([screens](../screens.md#setup)); where it differs from this page, build this page.

Sing Sing Karaoke Astoria is the first venue onboarded this way; West 4 keeps its demo seed and never needs the wizard, but the end-to-end test also runs it from scratch.

### Who runs it

- **Our Console makes the venue and invites its owner.** Today a venue exists only through the seed, the load-test setup and SQL, and `stripe:create-account` makes the Stripe account by hand. Phase 1 adds one Console action, **New venue** (our staff, signed in to the Console, audited): it takes the venue's name, the organization's legal name (or picks an existing organization, for a second venue of the same owner), a short web name (the slug), and the owner's name and email. It creates the `organizations` row (when new) and the `venues` row with `time_zone` America/New_York, the 6:00 AM cutover and the New York rule pack (the only one in phase 1; the address step confirms it), the core modules on, the modules every plan includes allowed and off (D129), and every other module allowed by nothing until a plan is chosen, and an owner membership in the `invited` state. Nothing else is filled in: no hours, rooms, prices or contacts.
- **The owner's invite** is emailed from the Console ("Send the owner's invite"; "Send again" makes a new link and ends the old one). The link lasts 7 days. Opening it runs the existing enroll path (an emailed code, then a passkey or an authenticator), and the owner lands on **Set up your venue**. The owner sets their own 6-digit PIN there as any owner does; we never set or see it.
- **Only the venue's owners run the wizard.** A manager signed in sees the checklist read-only ("Your owner is setting up the venue · N of 12 done") and can already work in the Admin sections a manager may edit. A second owner of the same organization can continue where the first stopped.
- **Our staff never fill it in.** With a support grant the owner approved, our staff can read the wizard's state to help on a call, as with any Admin screen; they can't save it.

### How it behaves

- **One step at a time, any order.** The left rail lists the 12 steps with a mark each: done, needs attention (amber), skipped, or not started; the header reads "Step N of 12" and "N of 12 done". The owner may jump to any step from the rail. Each step's summary line under its rail button is read from the saved rows, never from the wizard's own memory, so the wizard, Admin and the Console's venue list always agree (canvas note 10).
- **Save and resume.** Each step saves when the owner presses Continue or **Save and finish later**, through the step's existing route (below); a step half filled is kept as the owner left it in the browser only until they leave, and nothing reaches the venue until saved. Signing in again, on any device, opens Set up your venue at the first step not yet done until the wizard is finished, then Admin as usual. Admin → Setup keeps the checklist after that.
- **Skip for now.** Every step has **Skip for now**. A skipped required step stays on the checklist as "Needed before you open"; a skipped optional one as "Optional · you can do it later in Admin → …". A step the plan doesn't use is skipped by itself and shows why ("Skipped · no private rooms on the Bar plan").
- **Ready to open.** When every required step is done, the checklist reads **Ready to open** and the last step offers **Finish setup**. Finish setup stamps `venues.setup_completed_at` and, after a confirm, turns on the guest-facing modules the owner ticks (Online booking, Guest texts once texts are approved, the website), through the same `PATCH /modules/{id}` Admin → Features uses. A venue that isn't ready can't press it, and the screen lists what's missing with a link to each step ("Step 3 · Stripe needs your bank details · Go").
- **The first live night isn't a button.** Going live still follows the go-live runbook (S-12, M9-16): Finish setup only means the venue's own facts are in.
- **Validation** is the route's own: every settings save is checked against the venue's rule pack (spec 03), a room's capacity must be a whole number the owner enters, money is entered in dollars and cents and saved as integer cents, times in the venue's time zone. An answer the rule pack refuses shows the route's reason under the field. Empty is always allowed for a fact the venue hasn't decided; the screen shows the field's "not set" hint and never fills in a number for the venue.
- **Languages.** Every word is in the staff app's English and Spanish catalogs; the owner's own language setting picks one. The glossary's words apply ("Rooms", "Deposits & cancelling", "Put a card on the tab").
- **Read-only Admin.** The wizard is an Admin screen: once a venue has a plan and that plan's payment has failed past D94's 14 days, its saves answer `403 admin_read_only` like Admin's, with the same exceptions.

### Step details

**1 · Your venue.** The address is a street address the owner types; it confirms the rule pack and time zone the Console set and never changes them by itself (moving the time zone moves every business date, spec 03, so a venue outside New York is a Console job and refused here with "This address is outside New York · contact us"). The legal name goes to `organizations.legal_name`, which Stripe's form starts from. **The EIN is never asked or stored by us**: the owner types it into Stripe's own form (step 3) and into Twilio's own console when registering the venue's texting brand (step 11). The booking inbox gets a copy of each new online booking, change, cancellation and party enquiry, with no card data; empty sends none (the board and Messages still show them).

**2 · Your plan.** Three choices from the [blueprint](../blueprint.md#proposed-plans), named as there, with what each turns on in phase 1:

| Plan | Price shown | Includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bar | Read from Stripe (`west4_plan_bar`) | Bar tabs & quick sale, Bar mode, Bar screen & tickets, Team & tip pool, Guest texts, Reports, Cash discount, the core modules |
| Rooms | Read from Stripe (`west4_plan_rooms` plus `west4_plan_room` per room) | Everything in Bar, plus Rooms & room clock, Online booking, Deposits on bookings, Ordering from the room, Room tablets, Packages & specials, Song system control, the waitlist, Keep the card behind the bar, Pay my share |
| Rooms + Kitchen | Read from Stripe (`west4_plan_rooms_kitchen` plus `west4_plan_room` per room) | Everything in Rooms, plus Kitchen & food (phase 1's narrow module, [D98](../decisions.md): no kitchen screen or coursing yet; the card says so) |

**Every plan** also includes the Website, Safety & ID records, Marketing texts and Paid on outside card machine ([D129](../decisions.md)): always allowed, off until the owner turns them on, and our Console can't take them away (it shows "Every plan"). The step says so under the three cards.

The recommended plan follows "What you run". Prices are read from Stripe by lookup key at page load and never written in our code ([D94](../decisions.md)); if a key is missing the card reads "Not available yet · we'll email you when it is" and Start plan is off. The **promo code** field checks the code against our platform account's active Promotion Codes (`GET /v1/promotion_codes?code=…&active=true`) and shows what it gives ("First month free"); a code that doesn't exist or has expired reads "That code isn't valid". The founder makes the codes in Stripe (a Coupon of 100% off for 1 month, [D114](../decisions.md)); our code never names one. **Start plan** creates a Stripe Checkout Session in subscription mode on our platform account (the billing key; the organization's billing Customer; the plan's Price and the per-room Price with the venue's room count, 0 on the Bar plan; the promotion code as a discount; an idempotency key per attempt; never inside a transaction) and sends the owner to Stripe's hosted page, so their card never touches our servers. `checkout.session.completed` and `customer.subscription.updated` on the Our billing endpoint write `venue_subscriptions`, and the Console's allow-list is set from the plan. Choosing a plan also switches on every module it includes ([D121](../decisions.md)): all of them for a first plan, only the added ones when the plan changes, each after what it needs; a module whose own needs aren't met yet (Kitchen & food before its printer and allergy notice) stays off, chosen **on once ready** ([D129](../decisions.md)): it turns on by itself once they're in. The owner turns any module off afterwards in Admin → Features, and the same plan again never turns it back on; the step shows "Waiting for Stripe…" until the event arrives and the reconciler checks a session left open. Changing the plan later is Admin → Payments → Our plan, through Stripe's portal. This replaces D94's "started only by the ops command" for onboarded venues; `billing:subscribe` stays for our staff.

**Choose your features ([D124](../decisions.md)).** Once the plan is active, step 2 shows **Choose your features**: Admin → Features' own list, every module the plan allows with its switch, its needs and the "These turn off with it" confirm, then the Choices (Drinks rung at the bar print a ticket, Texting, and how approvals are decided). Each switch saves at once through `PATCH /modules/{id}` or its settings key, so the step and Admin → Features always agree; the step's summary reads "N features on". Nothing here is required: every module starts as the plan switched it, and the owner changes it later in Admin → Features. What a POS needs to work isn't listed, because it's always on: Reports & accounting, Bar tabs & quick sale, the menu, payments and receipts, Close the night and the Z report, team, roles and PINs, and the rest spec 03 names. Step 7 also offers **Paid on outside card machine** ([D126](../decisions.md)), off until the owner turns it on. **On once ready** ([D129](../decisions.md)): a module whose own needs are set later in the wizard can still be chosen here. Kitchen & food before its allergy notice (step 8) and kitchen printer (step 12) shows **On once ready** beside Off; choosing it records the module as wanted (`PATCH /modules/kitchen` with `{ state: "on", when_ready: true }`, `venue_modules.wanted_at`) and the module stays off, with the line "Turns on when the allergy notice and a kitchen printer are set" (naming only what's missing). While it's wanted, steps 8 and 12 ask for the notice and the kitchen printer (needed before Ready to open), and it turns on by itself as soon as both exist (a `kitchen` save, a kitchen printer added or moved to the kitchen station) or at Finish setup. Off forgets it. The server never has it on without them. Kitchen & food is the only module whose needs come later today.

**3 · Card payments.** The **Connect with Stripe** button, [Stripe setup](06-stripe-setup.md) step 2: the API creates the organization's Accounts v2 account if it has none (step 1 there, with the legal name from step 1 here and the owner's email; idempotency key per organization), then an Account Link, and sends the owner to Stripe's hosted onboarding; no key is ever pasted. Stripe's form takes the EIN, the bank details and the owner's ID. Back in the wizard the step reads the account: "Connected · card payments on" when `account.updated` says card payments are enabled, "Stripe needs more from you · Continue with Stripe" otherwise. A second venue of the same organization reuses the account and shows "Connected through Sing Sing Karaoke LLC". Payment-method domain registration and the Terminal Location follow automatically once connected (steps 4 and 10 there).

**4 · Rooms and prices.** The same forms as Admin → Rooms (`POST /rooms`, `PATCH /rooms/{r}`) and Admin → Prices (`PUT /settings/prices`). Capacity is the venue's fire-safety number, typed by the owner, never guessed. Minimum guests billed is two numbers, weeknights and Friday and Saturday; "Same minimum every day" (one tap) asks one number and saves it as both (D128; Sing Sing bills at least 3 all week). Happy-hour rates are time bands with their own rate. The room count changes the per-room fee only through the subscription's quantity (M8-15). The `prices` key is saved whole, so its first save in the wizard also asks the booking limits (shortest and longest booking, the largest party online) and the damage fee, each empty until typed; step 6 shows the largest party again. Admin → Rooms answers only while Rooms & room clock is on; the Rooms plans switch it on (D121), and if the owner has turned it off since, the step says to turn it on in Admin → Features. Before the kitchen opens (`kitchen.opensAt`), food reads "Kitchen not open yet" and is refused like a passed last order (O-06).

**5 · Hours.** `PUT /settings/hours` (weekly hours and the house last call, never later than the rule pack's close), the kitchen's times (`kitchen.opensAt`, new and optional: empty means the kitchen opens with the venue; `kitchen.lastOrder`), and the age policy (`safety.allAgesUntil`, [D108](../decisions.md)), which the owner confirms even when it's the default "21 and over". The kitchen fields show only when the Kitchen & food module is allowed. A Food Protection Certificate holder must be on site while food is served and after the kitchen closes ([D120](../decisions.md)); the step says so under the kitchen times, and nothing in the software enforces it.

**6 · Deposits and check-in.** `PUT /settings/deposit` with the M5 deposit rule (a deposit or a card hold for online bookings, free-cancel hours, the late-cancel and no-show choice, grace minutes, big parties) and the largest party online. **At a walk-in check-in** offers four choices ([D112](../decisions.md), [D116](../decisions.md)):

| Choice | `deposit.walkIn` | At Check in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Take a deposit | `deposit` (the default) | Today's step 5 |
| Put a card on the tab | `cardOnTab` | The guest taps a card on the front-desk reader; it's saved on the room's check, nothing charged |
| Keep the card behind the bar | `cardBehindBar` | Staff take the guest's physical card, type the name on it and its last 4 digits; no reader, nothing charged; the card is given back at close-out |
| Nothing | `nothing` | Seat them |

The step uses Admin → Deposits & cancelling's form once the `deposit` key is saved. The first time, nothing is chosen for the owner, the walk-in choice included; "No deposit for online bookings" asks only the no-show grace minutes and the walk-in choice, and saves the rest as nothing kept and nothing charged (`on: false`, late `refund`, no-show `nothing`, no big-party rule). The largest party online (the `prices` key from step 4) shows again under the step. Until the gratuity is saved (step 7), Admin's policy preview says it will show then (O-07).

**7 · Money at the counter.** `pay.cardFee` in mode `discount` takes the rate (0 to 10%, empty is off) and, new, `foodIncludesFee`: whether food prices already include the card fee (true) or are cash prices the discount never applies to (false); it shows only with the Kitchen module allowed. The step repeats Admin → Payments' note that a cash discount waits on the venue's own legal check (Sing Sing's is with the lawyer, [open questions](14-open-questions.md)). Gratuity and the drawer model are Admin's own forms; a drawer change starts at the next business date as in Admin. Once the `pay` or `drawer` key is saved, the step shows Admin's own form (Card fee & gratuity, Cash drawers). The first time, each key is saved whole, so the step also asks the rest of it (the tip screen and its amounts, tip review, the tip pool, Pay my share, a room hold; the drawer's counts and approvals), every answer empty until chosen; a per-person drawer's questions show only with that model (O-09).

**8 · Menu.** Three ways in, all through the menu API: type items in Admin → Menu's editor, upload a spreadsheet in the importer's menu format (M9-04's mapping, run as a dry run the owner checks, "Looks right", before anything is saved), or send a PDF or photo for our team to type in (it lands in the Console as a request; nothing is read automatically). Pour sizes and Draft follow [D105](../decisions.md). The open points the importer can't decide (a combo's included drink, extra sauce) are listed for the owner to answer on the item. The allergy notice is the venue's own words; empty keeps Kitchen & food off (spec 16). The spreadsheet is a mapping file with the CSVs it names, or CSVs in the importer's own column names (a file with a `group` column is the choices); it runs as the venue ([D122](../decisions.md)), so a second upload of the same file adds only what's new. The PDF or photo is a `menu_source` file, and the Console lists the request until our team marks it done.

**9 · Team and contacts.** Invites go through `POST /team/invite` (name, phone, role, language); each person confirms their phone and sets their own PIN on their own phone (M1-23). The contacts are a new owner-only settings key, `contacts` ([D115](../decisions.md)):

```ts
type Contacts = {
  bookingEmails: string[];                 // up to 3; a copy of each online booking, change, cancel and enquiry
  onCall: { first: OnCall | null; second: OnCall | null };
  breach: { primary: string | null; backup: string | null };   // membership ids: an owner or a manager
};
type OnCall = { membershipId: string; days: number[]; from: string; to: string };   // the hours they answer, venue time
```

The venue's on-call responders are the venue's own people our on-call calls when an incident touches that venue (a stuck payment, an outage, a security alert); our runbooks read them from here. They don't replace our platform rota (`oncall_rota`, M8-17), which is our own staff and stays open in [to-do](../todo.md). The breach contact receives our breach notice (we tell the venue within 72 hours, S9's default) and runs the venue's side; until the lawyer answers S9, the notice goes to the owner and the breach contact. Only an active owner or manager can be chosen, and deactivating one clears their slot and puts "Needs attention" on the checklist.

**10 · Licenses.** The license register (M8-09, `GET`, `POST` and `PATCH /licenses`, files through `POST /files`), with its renewal reminders. The liquor license is required before Ready to open; a missing music license shows a warning and doesn't block.

**11 · Texts, website and your old system.** All optional. **Texts:** the venue texts from its own Twilio account ([D123](../decisions.md)). The step links the owner's guide ([texts-10dlc.md](../runbooks/texts-10dlc.md)): open a Twilio account, buy a number, register the brand and the service campaign in Twilio's console, make a messaging service with the number in it. Then **Connect your Twilio account**, the same panel as Admin → Phone & texts: the Account SID and Auth Token are pasted and **Check** reads the account's +1 numbers and messaging services (a read-only call; nothing is saved yet); the owner picks the number and the messaging service and **Connect** saves it: the server points the number's incoming texts at our signed webhook, puts the number in the messaging service, reads the service campaign's status and keeps the token encrypted (never shown again, never logged). The panel reads **Not connected**, **Connected · waiting for approval** (with **Check again**) or **Approved**; texts stay off until approved, and until then codes and invites go by email. Owner or manager, as in Admin. **Website:** the venue's site runs on a test address now ([D119](../decisions.md)); "Do you have a website and domain to move?" records the answer and, when yes, starts M9-09's move with our team. Both the answer and the export files below are requests in the Console (`setup_requests`). **Your old system:** the owner uploads the export files (bookings, deposits, guests and consents, menu, team), which our team runs through the importer (M9-01 to M9-06) as a dry run the owner signs; nothing is scraped.

**12 · Devices and checks.** Pairing and reader registration are Admin → Printers & devices' own flows (M1-15, M3-13, M3-14, M4-02); the step lists what's paired and links each. The song system is `none` unless a partnership exists. The M4-29 checklist runs here for every venue: the merchant category read from Stripe, each manager's Stripe Dashboard login and Tap to Pay phone. **Card readers** ([D129](../decisions.md)): a Stripe reader is needed only when the venue takes cards through Stripe's readers. With Paid on outside card machine on, the line reads "Cards on your own card machine" and a reader is optional (the step says one can be added any time), unless the walk-in choice is Put a card on the tab, which taps a card on the front-desk reader. Online payments (deposits, booking, Pay my share) still go through Stripe, so step 3 and the merchant category check stay required. Then the checklist and **Finish setup**, which also turns on what was chosen "on once ready" once its needs are in.

### What's required before Ready to open

On every plan: Your venue (name, legal name, address, guest phone), a started plan, Stripe connected with card payments on, hours and the age policy, at least one menu item, one manager besides the owner who has set a PIN, a first on-call responder and a breach contact, the liquor license, one receipt printer with a drawer, the merchant category check, and one card reader unless Paid on outside card machine is on (a reader is still needed with it when walk-ins put a card on the tab, D129). On the Rooms plans also: at least one room with a capacity and a room rate, and the deposit rule with the walk-in choice saved. With Kitchen & food allowed, the Kitchen module still needs its allergy notice and a paired kitchen printer to turn on (spec 16), but a venue can open without its kitchen; while the owner has chosen it "on once ready", the notice (step 8) and the kitchen printer (step 12) are needed too, and turning it off in step 2 drops them.

### Routes

- Console: `POST /v1/console/venues` (New venue), `POST /v1/console/venues/{v}/owner-invite` (send or send again), and `GET /v1/console/venues` gains each venue's checklist.
- Staff app: `GET /setup` (the checklist: each step's state, read from the rows), `POST /setup/finish`; `GET /plan/prices` (the three plans' Prices, read from Stripe by lookup key), `POST /plan/checkout` (the Checkout Session), `GET /plan/promo?code=` (checks a promotion code), `GET /payments/stripe/connect` (the step's state) and `POST /payments/stripe/connect` (the account when the organization has none, then the Account Link); `PUT /settings/contacts`. Every other save is an existing Admin route. Each new route has a principal test and a venue-wall test.
