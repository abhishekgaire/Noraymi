## Open technical questions

Thirty-five questions are open: 8 for Stripe, 5 for the founder, 2 for Playbox, 4 for the accountant, 14 for the lawyer, 1 for a PCI assessor and 1 for the health department. The ones marked "Kitchen" come from the [Kitchen and food](16-kitchen.md) spec, and those after them from going live first at Sing Sing Astoria ([D98](../decisions.md)). On Oct 11, 2026 the founder asked for every question that common practice or the venue's own website can answer to be settled, and every venue-by-venue choice to be a manager's setting ([D102](../decisions.md) to [D106](../decisions.md)); what's left here needs a person only the founder can name, a price, or an adviser, and the legal and tax questions keep their cautious defaults until the lawyer or the accountant answers. "Blocks" names the [milestone](../milestones.md) whose work depends on the answer. Until the answer comes, that milestone builds it as a setting with the cautious default, so only one question stops a milestone from starting: M4 needs the entity and our Stripe platform account. "Gate" marks the answers that must be in before the go-live gate.
