## Devices, printing and offline

Anything that must not be missed, like a room order or a ticket, keeps ringing, retrying or alerting until a person handles it.

**Devices at West 4**

| Where | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| Bar | The bar computer (desktop app: the bar POS, the bar orders screen and the song queue), a USB NFC badge reader, a receipt printer with the bar drawer on its kick port, and the Bar S710 card reader |
| Front desk | The front-desk computer (desktop app: the board, room tabs and the rest), a USB NFC badge reader, a receipt printer with the front-desk drawer on its kick port, and the Front desk S710 card reader |
| Rooms | 14 room tablets, one per room; 13 are online tonight, because Room 4's is off while the room is out of service |
| Around the venue | The Up next TV at the bar, and a dual-WAN router with a cellular backup ("Backup internet · on") |
| People | NTAG 424 DNA badges for Abhishek, Andy, Maya and Diego, and each person's own phone, paired as a staff phone |

Both readers are S710s with cellular on ([Stripe setup](06-stripe-setup.md)). Every device above is a `devices` row (badges are `staff_badges`), and the Console reads the same rows as Admin → Printers & devices, so both show 13 of 14 room tablets online, both readers online and backup internet on.

**Cash drawers at West 4.** Two drawers, each run as a house drawer that the manager on duty answers for: the bar drawer on the bar printer's kick port and the front-desk drawer on the front-desk printer. Cash goes into the drawer at the screen where it's taken, and the log names who took it ("Logged to Maya · bar drawer"). Admin → Cash drawers can switch to a drawer per person, and Close the night counts both drawers blind ([Money rules](05-money-rules.md)).

- **Pairing.** A manager makes a one-time code in Admin → Printers & devices; the device enters it and gets its own signing key. Revoking a device ends its sessions at once.
- **Room orders at the bar.** A ringing order shows on the bar POS and the bar orders screen until someone taps Accept, and an asked-to-wait order stays there too, still aging. It shows cyan when it arrives. Ages on screen: amber at 2 min, pink at 4 when the manager on duty is told; bar phones at 30 s; a text or call at 6; chime as backup. Color carries the alert because a chime can't be relied on at karaoke volume, and Mute silences the chime for 1 minute. The times come from `pos.orderAging`, and the board follows the same clock: an alert at 2 minutes, "on Andy's phone" at 4 and "texted Andy" at 6 ([Staff screens and the bar POS](10-staff-screens-bar-pos.md)). The order rides on the device's own channel, so it shows and chimes while the staff screen is locked, and the desktop app plays it even when the window isn't in front. The desktop app keeps the computer awake, starts at login, restarts itself through a watchdog, and updates only between business days. The server tracks which bar devices are connected, and if none is during opening hours, every bar-role phone buzzes at once. The bar screens also check for ringing orders every 15 seconds.
- **Sound when a room's time is up (P-05).** A paired bar or front-desk computer, in the desktop app or the staff app in a browser, signed in or locked, reads the board every 15 seconds and plays a sound once when a room's tile turns amber (Wrap up) and once more when it turns red (its time is past), with the room's name in a strip above the screen (never over a button) until someone taps it; a tap clears the name and stops the sound. Each step sounds once per room session: the computer keeps what it played, so a reload doesn't play it again, and a room already past when first seen sounds once, not twice. The setting is in Admin → Alerts & rules beside the amber minutes: **Sound when a room's time is up** (`alerts.roomSound`, on unless switched off) and its volume (`alerts.roomSoundVolume`, 0 to 100, 50 until set), read with the board so a change applies at the next read. Switched off, nothing plays or shows, and turning it back on plays only for rooms that reach a step afterwards. Room tablets and the guest web never play it. A browser may hold the sound until someone has touched the page since it loaded; the room's name still shows.
- **Tickets.** Accepting an order creates a print job for that station's printer. That covers every room order, whether a guest placed it or staff added it from a room tab or Open in the bar POS. Drinks the bartender rings on a bar tab or a quick sale print only while `pos.printBarDrinkTickets` is on ("Print tickets for drinks rung at the bar", off by default, D99), because the person who rang them makes them. Network printers ([Star CloudPRNT](https://star-m.jp/products/s_print/CloudPRNTSDK/Documentation/en/index.html), [Epson Server Direct Print](https://download4.epson.biz/sec_pubs/pos/reference_en/technology/server_direct_print.html)) ask the API for jobs every 5 seconds or less and confirm each one they print. The job id is the CloudPRNT `jobToken`, so a lost confirmation can be worked out. USB printers print through a desktop-app host: at West 4, the bar computer and the front-desk computer, which also drive their drawers. A host can have printers for more than one station, each printer's station chosen in Admin → Printers & devices (a bar computer with the bar's printer and the kitchen's cabled to it, [D127](../decisions.md)). On Windows the host writes raw through the print spooler (RAW datatype) to printers on a USB port only. A **network printer** on the venue's LAN (a fixed private IP and port 9100) is printed by a desktop-app host on the same network: the venue may name which computers relay it, by default any bar computer with the app open; one relay at a time holds a 10-second lease on it and claims only jobs nobody has been sent, so each job prints once. Each printer a host prints on has a **command set**: ESC/POS, or Star raster for the Star TSP100III, which understands only Star's raster commands; the ticket is then the same 32 columns drawn as dots ("STAR Graphic Mode Command Specifications"), and its drawer opens with the raster-mode drawer command. It is suggested from the printer's name and changed in Admin → Printers & devices. A job still unconfirmed after three polls raises `print_job.failed` and shows "Ticket didn't print · Reprint" on the bar orders screen and the bar POS, and a reprint prints "REPRINT 2", then "REPRINT 3" and so on (`print_jobs.reprint_n`).
- **Cash drawer.** The drawer opens through the receipt printer's kick port, only with a move behind it: a cash payment, a drop, a paid-out (approved when it's over the limit), a no-sale (PIN again and a reason) or a tip-out, never for a reprint and never in training (D89). Every opening is logged against the open drawer session and the person. With a drawer per person it opens only for the person whose session is in it, so two people taking cash at once need two drawers, or a tray swap when their shifts don't overlap.
- **Room tablets.** Each tablet is paired to one room and runs the guest web in managed kiosk mode: the clock, the running total from the server, the menu and call staff. It works online only and has no PIN pad and no help link. Between sessions it shows "Room available" and takes no orders. A new session or a room move gets a new room code, and codes never contain the room number.
- **Joining a room.** Guests join from their own phones: they scan the QR code on the wall and enter the room's 5-character code, or open the link in the host's Room code text, which joins them as the host; everyone else joins as a friend. A wrong code says so, and ten wrong codes for one room rotate its code and alert staff. A room that's closed says so. After a move the old code stops working, and joined phones show "You've moved to Room 11 · new code …". Once staff present the check, joined phones show "Your bill is ready · ordering is closed".
- **Room QR slip ([D124](../decisions.md)).** While Ordering from the room is on, every room start (a check-in or a seated walk-in) prints a slip on the front desk's receipt printer (print kind `room_qr`): the venue, the room, the host's name, a QR code of the room's join link with its code (`/v/{slug}/room/{roomId}?code=…`, which fills the code in and joins with one tap), the code in large letters, and "Scan to order and see your bill" in the venue's language (English or Spanish). The QR is the printer's own command (ESC/POS `GS ( k`, Star and Epson markup), never an image. **Reprint QR** on the room (DeskRoom, the bar POS's rooms list and the staff phone's room) prints the current code again, the new one after a change; each print is a `print_jobs` row and goes on the record. No slip while Ordering from the room is off. When the host's email is known (their booking's, or typed in the walk-in dialog's optional email field), the room start also emails the host the same QR and join link, "Scan to order and see your bill", in English or Spanish; emails aren't switchable, so it goes whenever there's an email and Ordering from the room is on, and Reprint QR and a code change offer **Email it again**. A venue can also run on its bar computers alone, with no staff phones, room tablets, badge readers or Up next TV (D126): nothing requires a staff phone.
- **The bill before paying (P-01).** **Print the bill** on a room's close-out (before and after Present the check) and on a bar tab prints the check as it stands on the station's receipt printer (print kind `bill`, the bar's at the bar computer, the front desk's elsewhere), laid out like a receipt so it prints on ESC/POS and Star raster alike: the venue, "BILL", the check number with the room or the tab, the guests, each line with room time so far, comps and voids, tax, the gratuity, the total, the deposit and payments so far, Left to pay, and "This is not a receipt"; with the cash discount on, the cash price under the total (and under Left to pay once part is paid). The amounts are the check's: the finalized revision once presented, otherwise what packages/rules works out now. It's in the venue's first staff language (English or Spanish). It's core, never switched off; it can be printed again as often as asked, each one a new `print_jobs` row ("REPRINT 1", 2 and on) in the audit log. A printer that's offline shows the usual print error, with Reprint.
- **Staff phones.** Each staff phone is a device of kind `staff_phone`, so offboarding revokes it. The staff app installs to the home screen and gets push notifications for calls, runs ready for a runner, wrap-up alerts and orders for their rooms; bar-role phones also buzz for an order ringing 30 seconds, and managers' phones alone get approvals, help alerts and orders ringing 4 minutes. On iPhone, web push works only after the app is added to the home screen, so setup walks staff through it.
- **Up next display.** In bar mode, the Up next TV at the bar is a device of kind `up_next_display`. It shows who's singing now, the next 5 singers and a QR code to join, follows `song_queue.updated`, and never shows phone numbers ([Song systems and texts](11-song-systems-texts.md)).
- **Safety.** Check-in records the ID check in `id_checks`. The board shows a live headcount, from check-ins, walk-ins and a door counter at the front desk, against `safety.occupancyLimit`, and warns at 90%. West 4 hasn't entered a limit, so the board shows "Limit not set · Admin → Safety" and never a made-up number. The room page on guests' own phones has a discreet "Need a manager, privately?" link that alerts only the managers' phones and never shows on the room's screens.
- **Router.** The dual-WAN router is a device too. We pick a model with a vendor cloud API that reports whether it's on the wired line or on LTE; for a router without one, the server infers LTE from the carrier behind the bar computer's public IP. Admin and the Console show "Backup internet · on" while the backup is ready, and the board's amber banner shows only while the venue is running on it. Its failover is tested every month: due a calendar month after the last test, with one reminder to the managers when it falls due, and each result (passed or failed, and how long the switch took) kept on the router's page. The server reads the router on the 30-second heartbeat; the first supported maker is Peplink, through InControl 2's documented API ([D90](../decisions.md)).
- **Supported hardware.** Setup and the Console offer only models that work with this design: a Stripe S710 at every pay point, the only reader with cellular, at $10 a reader a month, with S700 and WisePOS E readers labeled "no cellular backup"; Star CloudPRNT and Epson Server Direct Print printers (a CloudPRNT model, such as the TSP100IV or mC-Print3: the TSP100III has no CloudPRNT), USB printers on a desktop-app host on macOS or Windows, and network printers on the venue's LAN printed by a desktop-app host (raw on port 9100), each ESC/POS or Star raster ([D127](../decisions.md)); a dual-WAN LTE router on a different carrier from the readers; and a USB NFC reader with NTAG 424 DNA badges for each shared screen.
- **Clocks.** Tablets and phones tick room clocks using the offset they measure from `server_time`, and take the running total from the server every minute. Each device reports its clock in heartbeats, and one more than 30 seconds off raises an alert. Billing always uses the server's times.
- **Heartbeats.** Every device checks in every 30 seconds, and heartbeats never go into the audit log. Two minutes of silence during opening hours raises `device.offline`; Stripe treats a reader as offline on the [same 2-minute rule](https://docs.stripe.com/terminal/payments/collect-card-payment?terminal-sdk-platform=server-driven). Device alerts go to the venue's manager, grouped into one "venue offline" alert when the whole venue drops, and page us only when a money path is at risk.
- **Training mode.** Training can be turned on for one device, such as a new hire's phone, as well as per person (Admin → Team → Training mode). While it's on, the screen shows the band "TRAINING · not real money", check numbers start with T- ("T-0012"), card steps run on a simulated reader in Stripe's sandbox, and the drawer never opens.

**Outages**

The board, the bar POS and the bar orders screen show one banner for each state below. The board's footer always shows the connection and the last sync ("Online · synced 4 s ago") and never says "works offline", because nothing that writes does.

| State | Banner | What staff can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On backup internet: the line is down and the router is on LTE | Amber: "On backup internet · card readers may take up to 2 min to switch" | Everything; a card payment in those minutes may show "Checking with Stripe · don't retry" or "Reader offline" |
| Offline: the line and LTE are both down, or our cloud is | Pink: "Offline · read-only · orders queue with an offline code" | See the board, open tabs and the menu; keep working the orders already ringing; queue new rounds with an offline code, which the bar POS marks "queued · not charged" |
| Back online | "Confirm replayed orders (3)" | Accept each replayed order, which lands as asked to wait |
| Stripe reports trouble | "Stripe is having trouble · card payments may fail" | Take cash when a card fails |
| Twilio reports trouble | "Texts are delayed" | Call instead; a room-ready text that fails shows "Not delivered · Call" |

Our vendor-health checks raise the last two from Stripe's and Twilio's status and our own error rates.

- **Offline and queue mode.** When the venue is offline, the cloud stays the only writer. The desktop app shows the board, open tabs and the menu read-only under the pink banner, and keeps ringing the orders it already has without a PIN. To queue new orders, staff enter an offline code that the desktop app checks itself: a time-based code from a per-device secret set up while online and kept in the operating system's keychain, or one-time codes printed in advance, valid only for that device and business date. A manager reads the code from their phone, which caches each paired device's upcoming codes while online so it works even when our cloud is down, or from a sealed card. It opens queue mode until the connection returns or 4 hours pass; every queued order records a staff name, each round shows "queued · not charged", and voids, refunds and the drawer stay locked. Staff phones on cellular keep working online, but they don't see the bar computer's local queue until it replays. Room tablets are online only, so the Console has no offline-tablet switch.
- **Replay.** Each queued order gets an `order_id` made on the device, and on replay the server checks it again: the session is still open, the check isn't finalized or paid, the alcohol window is open and the business date matches. Every replayed order lands as `held`, shown as asked to wait under "Confirm replayed orders", until a bartender accepts it against the tab, so an order also rung online during the outage isn't charged twice. An order that fails the checks goes to Review after outage instead.
- **Review after outage.** Close the night gets a "Review after outage" list for a manager: every order taken offline and its payment, with the ones that failed the replay checks first, and any break-glass card payments still waiting in Unmatched payments.
- **Break-glass card.** Close the night has a one-page card to print and keep at each desk: when our cloud is down, take cards with Tap to Pay in Stripe's Dashboard app on the manager's phone, and they appear in Unmatched payments for matching afterwards ([Stripe setup](06-stripe-setup.md)); if Stripe is down too, take cash.
