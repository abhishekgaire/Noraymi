# The whole venue went quiet (manager alert)

**Rule:** `venue-offline` · tells the venue's manager, **never pages us** on its own.

**What fired.** Every device at the venue went quiet at once, so M1-16 grouped it into one "venue offline" alert instead of one per device.

**For the manager.** It's the internet or the router. The router should switch to cellular backup; if it doesn't, restart it. Staff screens keep working offline, and the break-glass card covers card payments.

**When it becomes ours.** The readers going with it pages us as [readers-offline](readers-offline.md).
